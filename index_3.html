About

Thesis project 2025 · R&D project

Design & development of a high altitude long endurance UAV

Design, development, and experimental validation of a high-altitude long-endurance UAV, from conceptual sizing through prototype fabrication and flight testing. Full aerodynamic, structural, and stability analysis performed using CFD, FEA, and FSI. MDO framework applied to optimize aerodynamic efficiency and structural weight. Avionics integration includes autopilot, autonomous path planning, and energy-efficient flight strategies including thermal soaring. Analysis demonstrates capability for sustained operation at altitudes up to 10,000 m. Successfully prototyped and flight tested.

Solar-powered flying wing UAV

Conceptual design, detailed CAD modelling, and aerodynamic–structural evaluation of a solar flying wing UAV for multi-day endurance operation at medium altitude. Full CFD, FEA, and FSI analysis performed using SolidWorks Flow Simulation and Structural Solver, with aerodynamic stability evaluated in OpenVSP and XFLR5. Avionics integration includes autopilot system, autonomous navigation, and thermal soaring strategies for extended endurance beyond battery limits.
